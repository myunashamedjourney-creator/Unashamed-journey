THE FIVE-PART REBUILD

Five areas. One man.

Growth is not linear. When life shifts, come back to the circle and rebuild what needs attention.

01

Faith

Find an anchor deeper than the season you’re in. Hope, meaning, prayer, Scripture, surrender.

02

Mind

Name what is happening inside you. Fear, anger, shame, thought patterns, masks, avoidance.

03

Body

Rebuild agency through action. Train, walk, sleep, eat better, keep promises to yourself.

04

Identity

Stop letting your past, job, father, or failures define you. Decide who you are becoming.

05

Relationships

Show up differently. Repair, communicate, ask for help, be present, become safe and dependable.
