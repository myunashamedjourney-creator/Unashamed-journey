THE WORK

Faith is not pretending life is easy. It is knowing what you are anchored to when life stops being predictable.

WHEN THIS AREA DRIFTS

  • Trying to control every outcome because surrender feels unsafe.
  • Only turning to God when life is already on fire.
  • Treating faith like performance instead of relationship.
  • Letting disappointment convince you that God is absent.

“You do not need control over everything to have an anchor in something.”

Practical rebuild
