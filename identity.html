THE WORK

Identity is the answer to a hard question: when the roles, money, applause, mistakes, and history are stripped away, who are you choosing to become?

WHEN THIS AREA DRIFTS

  • You only feel valuable when you are useful to somebody.
  • Your job, paycheck, performance, or approval becomes your whole sense of worth.
  • You keep wearing old masks because they once helped you survive.
  • You talk about the past like it has already decided your future.

“Your past can explain you without controlling you.”
