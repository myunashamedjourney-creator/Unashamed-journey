The Unashamed Journey framework

THE REBUILD
CIRCLE.

Five connected areas. Not five boxes to check. Not five steps you have to complete in order.

Start where the work is

YOU DO NOT HAVE TO START AT 01.

Take an honest look at your life. Start where you need it. Come back when another area needs attention.

You don’t have to begin with Faith because it says “01.” You don’t have to fix every area of your life at the same time either.

Take an honest look at your life.

Maybe your faith is solid, but your marriage feels distant. Start with Relationships.

Maybe everything looks good from the outside, but your mind never shuts off and you’re constantly expecting the worst. Start with Mind.

Maybe you’ve stopped taking care of yourself physically. Start with Body.

Maybe you’ve built your entire identity around work, money, achievement or what other people think of you. Start with Identity.

The five areas are connected, so working on one will often expose another.

The point isn’t to follow the circle perfectly. The point is to become self-aware enough to recognize where you need to work — and then actually do something about it.

Start where you need it. Come back when another area needs attention.

How the circle works

FIVE AREAS.
ONE WHOLE LIFE.

Each area has its own work, but none of them lives in isolation. The work eventually touches everything.

01 / FAITH

RELATIONSHIP, NOT PERFORMANCE.

Faith is the foundation — rebuilding a relationship with God underneath hurt, doubt, bad examples, and religious performance. It starts with honesty, not earning your way in.

Read Faith ↗02 / MIND

UNDERSTAND WHAT IS HAPPENING INSIDE YOU.

Learn to recognize old survival patterns, fear, anger, masks, assumptions, and reactions before they make the next decision for you.

Read Mind ↗03 / BODY

BUILD THE CAPACITY TO SHOW UP.

Your body is not a trophy. Strength, health, energy, mobility, rest, and discipline help you carry the responsibilities and relationships that matter.

Read Body ↗04 / IDENTITY

KNOW WHO YOU ARE BECOMING.

Build your identity deeper than work, money, appearance, relationships, achievement, or approval — because all of those things can change.

Read Identity ↗05 / RELATIONSHIPS

WHERE THE WORK BECOMES VISIBLE.

The people closest to you eventually feel the work you are doing through your presence, honesty, repair, patience, leadership, and attention.

Read Relationships ↗

Faith gives you the foundation through relationship with God, not religious performance. Mind helps you understand what’s happening inside you. Body gives you the capacity to show up. Identity tells you who you are becoming. Relationships are where the work becomes visible.

Two parts. One system.

WHERE + HOW.

The Rebuild Circle helps you identify where the work is.

Face It. Own It. Change It. gives you a simple way to move through what you find.

The circle is not a scorecard. The method is not a shortcut. Together they give you somewhere honest to begin and a practical way to move.
