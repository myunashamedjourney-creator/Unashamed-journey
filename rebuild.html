THE REBUILD
CIRCLE.

Five connected areas. Not five boxes to check. Growth is not a straight line; the circle gives you somewhere honest to return.

Why a circle?

YOU KEEP
COMING BACK.

Life does not expose every weak spot at once. You can be strong physically and distant relationally. Clear in your identity and shaky in your faith. Growing in one area can reveal work in another.

01

NOT A SCORECARD.

The five areas are not a test you pass once. They are places to check in with as life changes.

02

NOT A STRAIGHT LINE.

You will revisit the same area more than once. That is not failure. That is what rebuilding looks like.

03

ONE WHOLE LIFE.

Faith, mind, body, identity, and relationships affect each other. Ignore one long enough and the strain eventually shows somewhere else.
