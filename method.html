How the rebuilding happens

FACE IT.
OWN IT.
CHANGE IT.

Three moves for getting unstuck without pretending your past did not matter.

03

The core idea

HONESTY.
RESPONSIBILITY.
ACTION.

The goal is not to blame yourself for everything that happened. It is to stop giving everything that happened permanent control over what happens next.

01FACE IT.

Name the pattern, pain, behaviour, fear, or reality without minimizing it or hiding behind a mask.

02OWN IT.

Ask what is yours now. Your response. Your habits. Your repair. Your next decision. Responsibility is not the same as blame.

03CHANGE IT.

Choose a practical move small enough to do and meaningful enough to create a different pattern.

Example

FROM PATTERN
TO PRACTICE.

FACE IT

“I keep coming home physically present but mentally somewhere else.”

OWN IT

“Work is demanding, but my attention at home is still something I can take responsibility for.”

CHANGE IT

“Tonight, the phone stays down for the first 20 minutes after I walk in the door.”

Apply it anywhere

FAITH. MIND. BODY.
IDENTITY. RELATIONSHIPS.

Different area. Same process: tell the truth, take responsibility, make the next move.
